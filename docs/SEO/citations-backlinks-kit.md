action plan. All facts match `frontend/src/config/business.ts` — keep them identical
everywhere (inconsistent NAP is the #1 thing that suppresses local rankings).

## 6. Backlinks — white-hat plays you can actually execute

Backlinks (other sites linking to you) are the biggest off-page ranking + GEO lever
after reviews. Realistic, doable plays:

1. **Supplier / manufacturer dealer pages** — equipment, filter, and chemical vendors
   you buy from often have "Where to buy" / "Find a dealer" / "authorized installer"
   pages. Ask to be listed with a link. (Easy, high-quality, topical.)
2. **Local partnerships** — realtors, property managers, HOAs, HVAC *installers* (you
   do the cleaning they don't), home inspectors, restoration companies. Trade
   "preferred vendor" listings/links. You already target these in the lead scanner —
   ask happy B2B clients for a website mention.
3. **Local press / HARO-style** — respond to journalist queries (Connectively/Qwoted,
   Help a B2B Writer) on indoor air quality, wildfire smoke, allergy season → earns
   news backlinks. LA wildfire/smog angle is very citable.
4. **Sponsorships** — local sports teams, schools, charity events almost always link
   sponsors from a .org page. Cheap, durable local links.
5. **Guest content** — write an "LA homeowner's guide to indoor air quality after
   wildfire season" for local blogs / community sites.
6. **Customer-driven** — your review-routing page already nudges happy customers to
   Google; also ask B2B customers (HOAs, property managers) to list you as a vendor
   on their sites.

## 7. GEO note (AI answer engines)

Citations + backlinks are also how ChatGPT/Perplexity/AI Overviews decide who to cite —
they reward businesses that appear *consistently across many authoritative sources*.
Your on-site GEO is done (llms.txt, schema, FAQ, AI-crawler access); this off-site work
is what makes engines confident enough to recommend you by name. Consistent NAP +
Yelp/Nextdoor/NADCA presence + a few quality backlinks moves the needle most.
